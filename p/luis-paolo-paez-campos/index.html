Company Profile

Logística Red Aduanera is the ground-freight and logistics company of Corporativo Red Aduanera Peninsular, a Yucatán-based group with more than three decades in foreign trade and supply-chain services. It moves general cargo, containerized freight and goods that require special handling, using tractor-trailers, 20- and 40-ft container chassis, refrigerated units and distribution vehicles of various capacities.

Security is central to its model: insured units, federally licensed drivers and 24/7/365 satellite monitoring give clients full visibility of their cargo. The company holds CAAT registration, is affiliated with CANACAR, holds the Socially Responsible Company (ESR) distinction and currently has 12 units available for new transportation services.

Professional Background

Luis Enrique Paolo Páez Campos is an Authorized Customs Representative (mandatario aduanal) for Corporativo Red Aduanera and heads the management of Logística Red Aduanera, the group's ground-freight company.

Perfil de la Empresa

Logística Red Aduanera es la empresa de transporte terrestre y logística de Corporativo Red Aduanera Peninsular, grupo yucateco con más de tres décadas en comercio exterior y servicios de cadena de suministro. Mueve carga general, carga contenerizada y mercancías que requieren manejo especial, con tractocamiones, chasises portacontenedores de 20 y 40 pies, unidades refrigeradas y vehículos de distribución de diversas capacidades.

La seguridad es el centro de su modelo: unidades aseguradas, operadores con licencia federal y monitoreo satelital 24/7/365 dan a sus clientes visibilidad total de su carga. La empresa cuenta con registro CAAT, está afiliada a CANACAR, tiene el distintivo de Empresa Socialmente Responsable (ESR) y dispone actualmente de 12 unidades para nuevos servicios de transporte.
