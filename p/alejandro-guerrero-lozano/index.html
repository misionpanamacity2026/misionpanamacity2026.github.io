Organization Profile

For more than 40 years, INDEX Yucatán has represented the export-oriented manufacturing companies operating in Yucatán, serving as their institutional voice before federal, state and local authorities. Its members span textiles and apparel, furniture, electronics, aerospace, automotive, jewelry, medical devices and marine manufacturing, including luxury yachts and boats. Together they generate billions of U.S. dollars in exports, approximately 85% of them destined for the United States.

INDEX Yucatán also acts as a private-sector soft-landing partner for companies evaluating Yucatán as an investment destination, supporting their due diligence and providing guidance on the regulatory, customs, fiscal and operational environment required to establish manufacturing operations in Mexico.

Professional Background

Alejandro Guerrero is a Public Accountant from the University of Yucatán with an MBA in International Business and Senior Management from Universidad Anáhuac, and executive training at Anáhuac and MIT, the University of Wisconsin, the American Management Association and Tec de Monterrey. Since 1995 he has served at Monty Mexico — the Mexican subsidiaries of Hong Kong–based Monty Group, which produce garments for the U.S., Canadian, European and Asian markets — where he is Managing Director. He chaired the Board of INDEX Yucatán in 2010–2012 and 2022–2025, is a former President of Yucatán's Business Coordinating Council (CCE), has been a National Counselor of INDEX Mexico since 2008, and sits on the boards of CANAIVE and CANACINTRA. In 2010 the U.S. Embassy named him Young NAFTA Leader for Mexico.

Perfil del Organismo

Desde hace más de 40 años, INDEX Yucatán representa a las empresas manufactureras de exportación que operan en Yucatán y es su voz institucional ante las autoridades federales, estatales y municipales. Sus socios abarcan textil y confección, mueble, electrónica, aeroespacial, automotriz, joyería, dispositivos médicos y manufactura náutica, incluidos yates y embarcaciones de lujo. En conjunto generan miles de millones de dólares en exportaciones, aproximadamente 85 % con destino a Estados Unidos.

INDEX Yucatán también actúa como socio privado de soft landing para empresas que evalúan a Yucatán como destino de inversión, apoyando su proceso de due diligence y orientándolas sobre el entorno regulatorio, aduanero, fiscal y operativo necesario para establecer operaciones de manufactura en México.
